## THE UPCOMING EXAM FORMAT (authoritative - from the instructor, via students; build everything around THIS)
Computer-based, answers typed into text boxes. 50 marks total, 5 questions; each (except Q4) has two parts totalling 10 marks.
- Q1A Argumentative essay, 250 words - 6 marks
- Q1B Paraphrase a paragraph - 4 marks
- Q2A Email writing (complaint, professional request, enquiry, etc.) - 6 marks
- Q2B Questionnaire on a given topic: exactly 4 open-ended + 4 closed-ended questions - 4 marks (1/2 mark each)
- Q3A Interpret a graphic/bar graph/table (read the question carefully: answer what it asks) - 5 marks
- Q3B Summarise a paragraph: write a title, identify and highlight/underline the keywords in the answer, no personal opinions/details - 5 marks
- Q4 Report writing: "investigate" a given situation and write a report (title, abstract/executive summary, introduction, methodology/methods, findings, conclusion, recommendations) - 10 marks
- Q5A Given an abstract, identify and WRITE OUT specific parts of it (e.g. "write the statement of purpose", background, methodology, results/findings, conclusion/implications) - 4 marks (4 parts, 1 each)
- Q5B Rewrite a grammatically incorrect paragraph in full, correcting every error, and underline/highlight each correction - 6 marks
NOT in the exam: citations/referencing (not in rubric), article writing, flowcharts. Duration: assume 2.5 hours (as in past papers) and say so.
Past papers (below) had an older 7-task format; reuse their tasks where they map to the new format, but structure all mocks, priorities and strategy around the format above.
