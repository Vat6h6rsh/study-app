# WP exam format (from instructor, via group chat)

Computer based, typed into text boxes. **50 marks**, 5 questions; each (except Q4) = 2 parts, 10 marks.

| Part | Task | Marks |
|---|---|---|
| 1A | Argumentative essay, 250 words | 6 |
| 1B | Paraphrase a paragraph | 4 |
| 2A | Email (complaint / professional / enquiry…) | 6 |
| 2B | Questionnaire: exactly **4 open + 4 closed** questions | 4 (½ each) |
| 3A | Interpret a graph/table: answer exactly what's asked | 5 |
| 3B | Summary: **title**, **highlight keywords**, no personal opinions | 5 |
| 4 | Report writing: "investigate" a situation (title, abstract, intro, methodology, findings, conclusion, recommendations) | 10 |
| 5A | From a given abstract, identify & write out 4 named parts (e.g. statement of purpose) | 4 |
| 5B | Rewrite a grammatically wrong paragraph in full; **underline/highlight each correction** | 6 |

- No citations needed (not in rubric). No article writing.
- Practice app: `study-app` → subject WP. Detailed notes: `docs/wp/concepts/`. Past-paper analysis: `docs/wp/00-exam-analysis.md`.
- Writing answers saved from the app go to `docs/wp/submissions/` → ask Claude "grade my latest WP submission".
