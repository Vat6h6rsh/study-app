# Phrase bank by task

Topic ID: `phrases`

## Concept and why

A phrase bank reduces planning effort. Choose a phrase because it expresses the intended relationship. Specific content must still come from the prompt.

## Step-by-step method

1. Identify the task and purpose.
2. Select one suitable phrase.
3. Replace every placeholder with precise content.
4. Remove padding and check grammar.

## Ready-to-use skeletons

| Task | Useful phrases |
| --- | --- |
| Essay | [Institution] should [action] because…; First,…; A further reason is…; Critics argue…; This concern is valid, but…; Therefore,… |
| Paraphrase | [Cause] led to [effect]; [Effect] resulted from [cause]; Although [qualification], [claim]; [Procedure] was used to… |
| Email | I am writing to request…; Could you please confirm…?; The order reference is…; Evidence is available…; I apologise for…; Thank you for considering…; Kind regards,… |
| Questionnaire | What difficulties, if any,…?; Describe your experience of…; What change would improve…?; During the past week,…?; Yes / No / Not applicable |
| Graph/table | Overall,…; X exceeded Y by…; The figure rose from… to…; It peaked at…; In contrast,…; The figures do not establish… |
| Summary | [Main subject] involves…; The passage explains…; [Essential relationship] while [qualification] |
| Report | This report investigates…; The review covered…; Evidence was collected through…; The findings indicate…; Within this sample,…; [Owner] should [action] by [date] |
| Abstract parts | Background: context/problem; Purpose: aimed to…; Method: surveyed/interviewed…; Results: found/showed…; Implications: suggests/may support… |
| Grammar | Rewrite complete sentences. Mark ==changed words== and changed punctuation. For deletions, highlight the repaired phrase. |

## Worked exam-style prompt

Write a polite follow-up on a room-booking request sent three days ago. Ask for confirmation before Friday.

## Fully worked model answer

Subject: Follow-up on study room booking request

Dear Library Team,

I am following up on my request sent three days ago to book a study room for our project group. Could you please confirm availability before Friday? If the requested room is unavailable, I would appreciate details of a suitable alternative.

Thank you for your assistance.

Kind regards,
Asha Rao

The subject identifies the purpose. The body supplies context and a specific action. The close maintains professional tone.

## Common mistakes that lose marks

- Leaving placeholders unchanged.
- Using therefore where no consequence follows.
- Repeating “it is important to note” instead of stating the point.
- Substituting a canned essay for task-specific reasoning.

Tip: Recall a phrase, then complete it with a new topic.

Trap: Proves is stronger than suggests. Match the strength to the evidence.

Remember: Clear ordinary words are often best.
