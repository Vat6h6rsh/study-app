# Academic style, cohesion and vocabulary

Topic ID: `style`

## Concept

Academic style is precise and appropriately formal. Unity means every sentence serves the controlling idea. Coherence is logical ordering. Cohesion uses references, repeated key terms and suitable transitions to connect ideas.

## Why it matters

The supplied synonyms list includes near-synonyms with different connotations. Notorious and renowned are not interchangeable. Prove is stronger than indicate. Choose meaning, not complexity.

## Step-by-step method

1. Choose one controlling idea per paragraph. Put familiar information before new detail.
2. Identify the relationship: addition, contrast, cause, example or conclusion.
3. Use a suitable transition only where helpful: furthermore, however, consequently, for example, overall.
4. Check pronoun references and consistent terminology. Replace unclear “this” with a specific noun phrase.
5. Choose a context-fitting synonym and check its grammar and strength.

## Template / skeleton

```text
get → obtain / acquire
show → indicate / demonstrate / display (choose according to evidence)
help → assist / support
decrease → reduce / decline (check transitive/intransitive grammar)
fair → impartial / objective
explain → clarify / elaborate
idea → concept / notion
start → begin / initiate
big → substantial / large
wrong → incorrect / inaccurate

Paragraph: [Claim]. For example, [support]. However, [qualification]. Therefore, [limited conclusion].
```

Tip: Replace every placeholder with task-specific content.

## Worked exam-style prompt

Rewrite formally and connect the ideas: “The team got lots of replies. The survey was small. It proves everybody loves the system.”

## Fully worked model answer

The team obtained numerous responses. However, the sample was small. The findings therefore indicate favourable views among respondents rather than universal approval.

## Why the model works

- Obtain fits data collection.
- However introduces a qualification.
- The conclusion is limited to the sample; prove and everybody are removed.

## Common mistakes that lose marks

- Moreover between unrelated claims.
- Therefore when no consequence follows.
- Obscure synonyms replacing clear ordinary words.
- Confusing findings, conclusions and implications.
