# Q2A · Formal email

Topic ID: `email`

## Concept

A formal email is a purposeful message to a specific reader. The subject identifies the matter. The opening explains why you are writing. The body gives enough detail for action.

## Why it matters

A busy recipient should understand the issue, identify the record and know what response is requested. Be polite and direct even when disappointed.

## Step-by-step method

1. Identify the reader, relationship, purpose and requested outcome.
2. Write a specific subject with an order or application reference where relevant.
3. Use “Dear [name/title/team],”. A full name or role avoids guessing gender.
4. Put the purpose first, then relevant dates, references, facts and available evidence.
5. Make a specific request with a reasonable deadline. Thank the reader, close politely and sign your full name.

## Template / skeleton

```text
Subject: [Action] — [specific matter/reference]
Dear [Name / Team],
I am writing to [purpose]. [Brief connection or introduction if needed].
[Relevant facts, dates and evidence].
Could you please [specific action]? [Reasonable date or next step].
Thank you for your assistance.
Kind regards,
[Full name and relevant role]

Complaint: I received [item] on [date], but [defect]. Please arrange [requested remedy].
Request/referral: Could you share [details]? If my profile is suitable, I would appreciate guidance on your referral process.
Enquiry: Could you confirm [dates, fees, eligibility and application steps]?
Apology: I apologise for [failure]. [Accept responsibility]. To prevent recurrence, I will [measure].
Follow-up: I am following up on [request] sent on [date]. Could you indicate the expected timetable?
```

Tip: Replace every placeholder with task-specific content.

## Worked exam-style prompt

Write a complaint email to a supplier. A monitor ordered for your office arrived with a cracked screen. Give order details and request a replacement.

## Fully worked model answer

Subject: Replacement requested for damaged monitor, order MON-284

Dear Customer Support Team,

I am writing to request a replacement for the monitor delivered to our office on 12 November under order MON-284. The screen was cracked when we opened the package, and the device has not been used.

The outer carton also had a dent near one corner. Photographs of the packaging and screen, together with the purchase receipt, are available for your inspection. We have retained the original packing materials.

Could you please arrange collection of the damaged monitor and confirm when a replacement can be delivered? We need the monitor for a training session on 20 November, so an update by 15 November would help us plan. Please also confirm that collection and replacement will involve no additional charge.

Thank you for your assistance. I look forward to your response.

Kind regards,
Asha Rao
Office Coordinator

## Why the model works

- The answer addresses all requested details and an actionable polite request directly.
- Details support the requested task and each paragraph has a clear purpose.
- The model follows the requested format with precise, complete sentences.

## Common mistakes that lose marks

- No subject or signature; slang; capitals and repeated exclamation marks.
- A long life history before the purpose.
- Threats, blame or a vague request such as “do the needful”.
- For a simulated email, describe the evidence you would attach. Do not imply the app has actually sent attachments.

Trap: A memorised answer on a related topic may still miss this prompt.

Remember: Check the finished answer against the exact instruction.
