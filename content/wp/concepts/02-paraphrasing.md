# Q1B · Paraphrasing

Topic ID: `paraphrase`

## Concept

A paraphrase restates the whole meaning in independent language at roughly the same length. It is not a summary. Preserve names, figures, technical terms, conditions and the strength of each claim.

## Why it matters

Synonym substitution alone leaves the source sentence frame intact. Changing “may help” to “will solve” or “associated with” to “caused by” changes meaning.

## Step-by-step method

1. Read twice and list the essential propositions.
2. Look away and explain the paragraph from your notes.
3. Change structure: split or combine sentences, reorder ideas, move a clause or change voice.
4. Change word form and the surrounding grammar: “the team analysed” becomes “the team’s analysis”. Use contextual synonyms.
5. Compare claims with the source. Check omissions, additions, copied frames, length and grammar.

## Template / skeleton

```text
Source: Although the service was free, some students could not access it because they lacked a suitable device.
Skeleton: [Obstacle] prevented [group] from using [service], despite [qualification].
Paraphrase: The absence of appropriate equipment kept some learners from using the service even though it cost nothing.
Voice: The team analysed the files → The files were analysed by the team.
Word form: The team decided → The team’s decision.
Reordering: After checking the files, staff replied → Staff replied after checking the files.
```

Tip: Replace every placeholder with task-specific content.

## Worked exam-style prompt

Paraphrase the paragraph. Preserve its meaning and approximate length. Change vocabulary and sentence structure without adding claims.

Source passage: A college uploaded all its reading material to a digital portal. Students could download chapters before travelling and study without a continuous internet connection. However, several scanned documents were incompatible with screen readers. The library therefore began replacing these files with accessible versions. Staff also provided a short guide explaining how to locate and download the readings. The change improved access only when both the documents and the instructions were usable.

## Fully worked model answer

By placing its course readings on an online platform, the college enabled learners to save chapters in advance and read them while disconnected. Access nevertheless remained difficult for users of screen-reading software because some scans could not be interpreted. Librarians responded by preparing compatible files and offering brief instructions for finding and saving them. Thus, the initiative made study easier only where learners could use both the material itself and the guidance supplied.

## Why the model works

- The answer addresses the stated procedure, purpose and qualifications directly.
- Details support the requested task and each paragraph has a clear purpose.
- The model follows the requested format with precise, complete sentences.

## Common mistakes that lose marks

- Changing only two words while copying the sentence structure.
- Dropping a negative, qualification, number or causal link.
- Replacing a technical term inaccurately.
- Condensing to half length or adding an opinion.

Trap: A memorised answer on a related topic may still miss this prompt.
