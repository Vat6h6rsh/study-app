# DM exam-week plan · 22–28 November
Topic ID: `examweek`

Exam: BCS ZC219, 28 November 2026, 50 marks, typed step-by-step answers.

## Start here on 26 November

Do the 26 November block today and the 27 November block tomorrow. Earlier days are a checklist for finding gaps, not an instruction to compress seven full days into two. Use Core practice, then the Slot 1 pattern filter and Slot 1 mocks when available. The reported Slot 1 pattern is a practice guide, not a guarantee of your paper.

Remember: At least 50% comes from the professor's seven groups: truth tables, induction, relations, posets, recurrences, graphs and MST. Graphs occurred in all five past papers; induction, posets and recurrences also recur. Relations remain core by explicit professor guidance.

## 22 November · Logic and proof basics

Read logic and proof techniques. Complete 5 full truth tables, including an implication expression, and 3 short proofs/counterexamples. Type intermediate columns. Check every row before comparing whole statements. Keep a mistake list.

## 23 November · Induction and sets

Complete 6 induction questions: 2 sums, 2 divisibility, 1 inequality, 1 strong or structural induction. Type basis, hypothesis, step, conclusion in each. Add 4 sets/functions questions; distinguish membership from subset and countability from finite size.

## 24 November · Relations and posets

Complete 5 relations/classes questions and 6 poset questions. Include equivalence proofs, partitions, Hasse covers, maximal/minimal, greatest/least, and LUB/GLB. Describe Hasse levels and covers in text. Test bounds only inside the stated poset.

## 25 November · Recurrences and graphs

Complete 6 recurrence questions: homogeneous distinct roots, repeated roots, and non-homogeneous trials including resonance. Show the characteristic equation, general solution and initial conditions. Complete 6 graph questions: degree sums, complements, coloring, paths/cycles, connectivity and a proof. Review one Kruskal and one Prim trace.

## 26 November · Slot 1 pattern and first timed mock

Practise one full Q1–Q5 route: implication truth table (5 marks), divisibility induction (5), sets (2), Hasse bounds/extrema (8), equivalence relations/classes, non-homogeneous recurrence, Kruskal MST. The remaining Q3–Q5 allocations follow the practice paper data. Do 2 extra recurrences and 2 MST traces, including tied edges. Sit one Slot 1 mock if available, otherwise Mock 1, using its stated practice duration. Self-mark every proof step and redo 5 mistakes.

## 27 November · Second mock and repair

Sit a different Slot 1 mock if available, otherwise Mock 2. Budget roughly 3 minutes per mark for a 150-minute practice paper, or follow the selected mock's shorter budget. After marking, redo 6 weakest questions without revealing. Cover any omitted core group with at least 2 questions. Review the cheatsheet and type one complete proof, recurrence and MST explanation from memory. Finish with light review and sleep.

## 28 November · Exam morning

- Review the mistake list and notation cheatsheet; avoid a full last-minute mock.
- Truth tables: T before F; implication is false only for T -> F; compare complete columns.
- Induction: correct starting index, all required bases, arbitrary k, justified step, quantified conclusion.
- Relations: universe first; reflexive, symmetric, transitive; classes disjoint and cover the universe.
- Posets: bounds inside the poset; maximal differs from greatest; every pair needs a join and meet for a lattice.
- Recurrences: homogeneous plus particular solution; check resonance and all initial conditions.
- MST: accepted edges in algorithm order, explain cycle/cut rule, exactly |V|-1 edges, final weight.
- Number every typed step; state the final result explicitly. Check the actual exam instructions for duration and allowed aids.

Trap: A matching formula or correct total alone does not demonstrate your reasoning. The app checks final answers; you still need the typed derivation in the exam.
